What we do

Services

Every coating is only as good as the surface beneath it. We handle both halves of the job, so nothing gets lost between trades.

01

Abrasive Blasting

Garnet, glass bead and grit blasting to remove rust, mill scale and old coatings, leaving a clean anchor profile ready for paint.

02

Soda & Gentle Blasting

Low-impact media for softer substrates such as timber, brick, aluminium and classic car panels, without warping or pitting.

03

Protective Coatings

Epoxy, polyurethane and zinc-rich systems applied to spec for corrosion protection on structural steel, tanks and machinery.

04

Spray Painting

Airless and conventional spray finishing for gates, trailers, equipment and commercial fit-outs, in the colour you need.

How we work

From rust
to finish

A straightforward process, with clear pricing up front and no surprises once the job starts.

  1. Assess

    We inspect the job, in person or from photos, and recommend the right media and coating system.

  2. Prepare

    Masking and blasting to the preparation level your coating needs.

  3. Coat

    Primer, build and topcoats applied within the right window to lock out moisture and corrosion.

  4. Inspect

    Film thickness and finish checked before handover, so you know it's done properly.
